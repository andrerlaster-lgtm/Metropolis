# Reference: Tempora (life simulator)

- **Repository:** https://github.com/Mofferato/tempora (play it at https://mofferato.github.io/tempora/)
- **What it is:** a browser life simulator across 12,000 years. You live one year at a time and carry on through your heirs, while the world simulates everyone else (countries, settlements, wars, migrations). Vanilla JavaScript modules bundled into one `index.html` by `build.js`, with no dependencies, like Metropolis.
- **Licence:** MIT (Copyright 2026 Mofferato and Tempora contributors). Ideas are free to use. Code copied from it must keep the MIT notice, credited in the README's Credits.
- **Looked at:** commit `562865a` (2026-10-17), on 2026-10-24, for Pocket Metropolis.
- **Not to use:** its optional Claude features (chat with characters, "play for me", AI-written events). Metropolis adds no AI features or paid APIs without Andre's approval.

## Ideas for Metropolis, most useful first

1. **Install as an app, and play offline.** Tempora ships a `manifest.webmanifest`, app icons and a small service worker (`sw.js`, written by `build.js`). On an iPad, "Add to Home Screen" then opens it full screen like an app. Metropolis has none of this yet.
   - **The change:** a manifest, icons and a network-first service worker generated by `build.mjs`.
   - **Watch out:** the site sits behind Vercel login protection, so the manifest link may need `crossorigin="use-credentials"`. It has to be tested on the real protected site.
2. **Effect previews on buttons.** Tempora's `src/delta.js` shows what an action will change before you tap it (chips like "+4 Smarts", "−$300") and the real change afterwards.
   - **Life Story:** Metropolis could show chips on its activities; the estimates are already in `ACTIVITY_GAINS` in `autopilot.js`.
   - **Elsewhere:** town decision choices and the Town hall duties (which already show one line).
3. **Auto mode for life events.** Tempora's auto-play (`src/auto.js`) has a setting for "when a choice pops up": ask me, play it safe, be bold, choose in character, or choose what serves my goal. Metropolis's child auto mode stops at every life event; this would let it carry on.
4. **Skip ahead until something needs me.** A fast-forward button that runs time until an event, a raid, a town decision or a new upgrade point needs an answer.
5. **A headless balance simulator.** Tempora's `tools/sim.js` plays many lives in Node and reports balance numbers. It has two modes: press every button, or play like an ordinary player (`--typical`). It also catches runtime errors.
   - **What it would answer for Metropolis:** run many towns for N days with and without auto mode, and report money, happiness, upgrade points earned, mayor duties done, and raids won and lost.
   - **Why it matters:** this is how to tune the skill tree and the mayor, whose balance is still unverified.
6. **Goals with a deadline.** Tempora's story director (`src/director.js`) offers "a goal worth chasing" with a target date, progress and a reward, plus story threads that run over several years. Metropolis could give the mayor goals like "reach 300 residents by 1860" or "open a clinic within two years".
7. **Elections and succession.** `src/politics.js` covers office ladders, elections, rulers' succession and revolutions. These fit the mayor ideas already in TODO: elections, rivals, an heir who must win the office.
8. **Dynasty.** `src/legacy.js` and `src/tree.js` cover a family tree view, dynasty traits that build up over generations, heirlooms and wills. They would deepen "how the child grows up decides the next mayor".
9. **A world map with fog of war.** The Atlas colours lands by realm, wealth or faith, keeps unknown lands in fog, and lets alliances pull friends into wars. It's useful for the planned espionage (fog of war) and multi-town diplomacy.
10. **One place for bonuses.** `Sim.addHook(name, fn, mode)` combines effects by multiplying, adding or running in turn. Metropolis now has bonuses from notable people, town upgrades, decisions and the mayor, each wired by hand; one shared combiner would make the next bonus a one-line change.
11. **Smaller ideas:** procedural music for each era (`src/sound.js`); Simple, Comfortable and Compact layout densities; pinned actions that repeat every year.
